MEMORIES

Written by:
Sharon S. Wasserman
Mom of Scott and Brett

FIRST HOUSE

The house on Benecia, our first house, his first go-round with putting in a sprinkling system (it worked), building a wall unit for the back bedroom (he used 2x4s and plywood and painted each drawer a different color...it had a silk screen set up that we made our first Christmas cards on), the sand box he built for Scott that drew all the neighborhood cats in spite of the cover he made (I think we have pictures of it with Scott and Jason playing with Tonka trucks) and the day Brett was born, Scott, Bogey and Tom on the hillside waiting for a call from the hospital. Grandpa Tom and Grandma Lucille were there too. My mom was probably at the hospital. We have a great picture of Scott holding 3 day old Brett sitting next to Grandpa Tom in the room off the kitchen. Another memory was your dad refinishing all the kitchen appliances while I was visiting Judy Harrington in the desert...a birthday surprise for me. We couldn't afford new ones, and I was delighted with the newly white oven and dishwasher.

Sharon S. Wasserman
San Diego, CA
