STANFORD MEMORIES

Hello, Guys-

My name is John Minoleti. I live up in the sticks near Redding. Your dad, Alex, Bill Merrifield, Chuck Keller, and a few guys ran around together when we were Freshmen at Stanford, and your dad, Alex, and Chuck Keller used to go up to McCloud to hunt deer with my family. Since McCloud was a Little Italy (my grandmothers lived there for 60 years and never learned to speak English: they never had to) some of the hunters spoke with an accent. So, Alex became "The Lidla One" and your dad was "The Bigga One," like in, "The Lidla One is here, but the Bigga One, he's a gone." Your dad go lost a couple times. He liked being up there just like he liked almost everything he encountered. His dress was Long Beach style with a concession regarding footwear.

When the rest of us went to the Fiji house your dad went to DU; he had too much dignity to go with us fools. So, I didn't see as much of Tom, and after we both began working a lot, it was just an occasional phone call. But I can tell from your list of "dadisms" that he never changed much. I'm sure he was in bliss having a little captive audience like you guys. You are very lucky. He got a kick out of just about everything and anything in life. But most importantly, like I told Alex, he was a kind and good person. That counts the most.

John Minoletti
Ono, California
